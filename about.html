CEO & Founder

Troy D. Hightower

Formed TDH International LLC in 2016 after retiring from Kern Council of Governments, the region's metropolitan planning organisation, as a Regional Transportation Planner. Over 30 years of global experience delivering innovative, timely and competitive solutions, and over 40 years of aviation-related experience as a pilot.
